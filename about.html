About the project

A blog for learning in public.

I started Small Signals because writing is one of the best ways to find out what I actually understand.

Every post begins with a question I find interesting. I read primary sources and credible reporting, take notes, and turn the result into a clear, useful explanation. The goal is not to sound certain about everything—it is to make difficult ideas easier to approach.

What you can expect

  • One focused idea per post.
  • Plain language, with important nuance kept intact.
  • Links to sources so you can continue exploring.
  • A steady archive built one note at a time.

If a topic makes you pause and think, it probably belongs here.

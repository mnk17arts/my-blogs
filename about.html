About the publication

A publication for learning in public.

I started F.Y.I. because writing is one of the clearest ways to discover what I genuinely understand.

Every note begins with a question I find interesting. I read primary sources, technical specifications, and research papers, take notes, and turn the result into a clear, grounded explanation. The goal is not to sound certain about everything—it is to make difficult concepts approachable without stripping away essential nuance.

What you can expect

  • One focused idea per post: deep coverage of a single concept, system invariant, or emerging architecture.
  • Rich visual explanations: custom SVG diagrams, comparison models, and callouts rather than walls of text.
  • Plain language: direct, engaging prose that respects technical rigor.
  • Primary citations: direct links to standards (3GPP, IEEE, NIST, RFCs) and papers so you can verify and explore further.
  • A steady archive: built one thoughtful note at a time without artificial publishing pressure.

If a concept makes you pause and think, it belongs here.
